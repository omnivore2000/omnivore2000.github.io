About Me

I’m an After Effects veteran, 3D generalist in Blender, storyboard illustrator, video editor and designer.

I’ve been working in the multimedia space for over 15 years. I started out as a web designer when Flash was big then moved solely into video. I picked up Cinema 4D in around 2009 and became a Blender convert in early 2025.

I’ve worked in small studios, big agencies and independently. I’m now based in Johannesburg working with Cape Town animation studio 3rd Floor. You can see our work at www.3rdfloor.tv
